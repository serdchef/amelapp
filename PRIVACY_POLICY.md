# Privacy Policy for Faziletli Ameller

**Last Updated: May 9, 2026**

Faziletli Ameller ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Location Data
To provide accurate prayer times based on your current location, the application requests access to your device's location (GPS).
*   **How it's used:** Your location is processed locally on your device to calculate prayer times using astronomical formulas or by querying a prayer time API.
*   **Storage:** We **do not store** your location data on our servers. It is used only while the app is active or for background notification updates.

### 1.2 Personal Data
We **do not collect** any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

## 2. Data Usage and Third Parties

*   **No Tracking:** We do not use any tracking cookies or third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel).
*   **No Advertising:** Our application is 100% ad-free. We do not share any data with advertising networks.
*   **External Links:** The app may contain links to external Islamic resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

## 3. Data Security
Since we do not collect or store your personal data on external servers, the risk of data breaches is minimized. All your settings (saved deeds, city selection) are stored locally on your device.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our app is safe for all ages and does not collect any data from children or adults.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
